Getting started

A few minutes, no Terminal.

The app's Setup window walks you through it and ticks each step off as it works.

Install and activate

Unzip AppleMailMCP, move it to Applications and open it. Paste the license key from your Gumroad receipt and click Activate.

Connect Claude

Click Connect for Claude Desktop, Claude Code, or both. Quit and reopen Claude Desktop so it picks the new tools up.

Allow Full Disk Access

In System Settings, turn on Full Disk Access for Claude, and for your terminal app if you use Claude Code. Quit and reopen it, then ask Claude about your latest email.

What Claude can do with it

Five apps, one connection.

Reading is on from the start. Anything that changes your data is off until you switch it on.

Mail

Search every account by sender, subject, date or the text of the message, read threads, and, if you allow it, flag, move or draft a reply for you to send.

Calendar

See what is on your calendars, find an event, and, if you allow it, add one.

Messages

Search your iMessage and SMS conversations and read a chat back.

Reminders

Read your lists and what is due, and, if you allow it, add a reminder.

Notes

Search your notes and folders and read a note in full.

Private by design

Your data is read on your Mac and handed only to the Claude apps you connect. AppleMailMCP sends none of it anywhere: no server, no account, no analytics.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Does my mail go to a server of yours?

No. AppleMailMCP runs entirely on your Mac. The only things it sends over the network are the license check to Gumroad (your key, nothing else) and the check for app updates.

Can Claude send email or messages for me?

No. With the compose switch on, Claude can open a filled-in draft in Mail; you read it and press Send yourself. Messages is read-only.

Why does it need Full Disk Access?

macOS keeps Mail, Messages and Notes private. Full Disk Access is how you let an app read them, and it belongs to the app that runs the tools: Claude, or your terminal for Claude Code.

Does it work with Intel Macs?

No, it needs Apple silicon.

Something isn't working.

Open the menu-bar icon, choose More → Copy Diagnostics, and reply to your Gumroad receipt with what you pasted. It contains setup details only, never your mail or messages.
