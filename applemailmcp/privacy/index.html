Privacy

AppleMailMCP keeps your data on your Mac.

  • Your mail, calendars, messages, reminders and notes are read on this Mac and passed only to the Claude apps you connect AppleMailMCP to, when they ask. AppleMailMCP itself sends none of that content anywhere.
  • License check: when you activate, and about once a week after, your license key and the product id are sent to Gumroad to confirm the purchase. Nothing else is sent.
  • Updates: the app checks michelstorms.com for a newer version. That request carries the app's version, as any web request carries your IP address. No usage data or analytics are collected. (Only in developer mode, which a purchased copy never uses, does the app also fetch from the source repository to compare versions.)
  • Diagnostics: "Copy diagnostics" puts version, setup and permission details on your clipboard, for you to paste into a support email if you choose. It contains no mail, messages, notes or other content.

Questions: write to the support address on the product page.
