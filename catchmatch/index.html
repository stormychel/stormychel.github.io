Questions

Frequently asked.

What can I use it with?

Material you have the right to use: your own recordings, music you bought on Bandcamp, and public-domain or Creative Commons sources like Archive.org. It converts the links or files you give it with open-source components, then hands the result to Apple Music, Apple TV or your Downloads folder. It doesn't get around DRM, and it isn't meant for services whose terms forbid downloading.

Do I need Apple Music or iTunes Match?

No. Turn on Downloads and the finished file lands in your Downloads folder, with no library involved. Apple Music with Sync Library, or iTunes Match, is what carries a track from your Mac to your iPhone, iPad and HomePod — useful, not required.

Why isn't it on the Mac App Store?

The App Store doesn't accept apps built on the open-source components this one needs. CatchMatch is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so macOS opens it normally.
