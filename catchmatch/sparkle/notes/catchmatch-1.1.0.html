New version 1.1.0 - Send pages from Safari, and no more Homebrew

- New Safari extension: press its toolbar button, or right-click a link, to send a page to CatchMatch. Pick Apple Music, Apple TV or Downloads, and the import starts straight away
- Setup installs everything CatchMatch needs by itself, in one click. No Homebrew, no Terminal
- Conversions use your Mac's built-in video encoder, so they finish faster
- The setup check only asks for access to Music when you press its button, and explains why first
- CatchMatch is now sold from the Orchard Outlaws store
