Native macOS utility

ClaudGet

Your Claude usage, at a glance. ClaudGet keeps current-session, weekly, model, and usage-credit limits visible in native desktop and Notification Center widgets.

  • Widget first
    Small, medium, and large layouts for the amount of detail you need.
  • Private by design
    Your Claude session remains in the app’s Keychain and is never shared with the widget.
  • Always current
    The host app refreshes usage and publishes a credential-free snapshot to WidgetKit.
