ClaudGet Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026. Applies to the ClaudGet macOS application sold through Gumroad.

Short version

  • No telemetry, no analytics, no tracking. The developer runs no ClaudGet server and receives nothing from the app.
  • The app talks to three places: claude.ai for your usage, Gumroad to verify your license key, and michelstorms.com to check for updates.
  • Your Claude session stays in your Mac’s Keychain. The widgets only ever see usage numbers.

Data the app sends

WhatWhereWhenWhy
Your Claude session cookie and organization identifierclaude.ai (Anthropic)When you sign in and on every refreshTo read your plan and usage limits. Anthropic also sees ordinary network information such as your IP address. Anthropic’s privacy policy applies.
License keyGumroad (api.gumroad.com)On activation, then about once a dayTo confirm the license is valid and not refunded. Gumroad’s privacy policy applies.
Update checkmichelstorms.comAbout once a day, and when you choose Check for Updates…To fetch the list of available versions (Sparkle). The request identifies only the app and its version; no system profile is sent.

The app does not send your prompts, conversations, name, email, or usage figures to the developer or anyone else.

Data stored on your Mac

  • Keychain: your claude.ai session, until you choose Disconnect.
  • App Group container (shared only with ClaudGet’s own widget): the latest usage numbers, and a local diagnostics log of refresh outcomes. Credentials are redacted from it.
  • Preferences: refresh interval, Dock visibility, trial start date, and license state.

None of this is uploaded. Disconnect removes the session and cached usage; deleting the app and its container removes the rest.

Purchases

If you buy ClaudGet, Gumroad processes the payment and holds your purchase record (email, order, license key). The developer sees what Gumroad shows sellers about a sale.

Children

ClaudGet is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

Meaningful changes are published on the ClaudGet page, with the date above updated.
