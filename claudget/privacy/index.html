ClaudGet Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ClaudGet (“App”) handles information on macOS. The developer is Michel Storms and can be contacted at the address below.

1. Information Processed

  • Authentication data: Claude web-session cookies obtained after you sign in. They are stored in your macOS Keychain and are not shared with the widget.
  • Account and usage data: your organization identifier, plan label, usage percentages, reset times, and usage-credit values returned by Claude.
  • Preferences: your selected refresh interval.
  • Diagnostics: local timestamps, refresh outcomes, usage values, and widget status. Credentials and response bodies are not intentionally logged.

2. Network Requests

When you sign in or refresh, the App connects directly to claude.ai. Your session cookie and organization identifier are transmitted to that service to authenticate and return account usage. Anthropic may also receive ordinary network information such as your IP address and device/browser request metadata. The developer does not proxy these requests and operates no ClaudGet account, analytics, advertising, or telemetry server.

3. Data Collection and Sharing

The developer cannot access information processed locally by the App and does not collect, sell, rent, or share it. ClaudGet contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. Data sent directly to Anthropic is handled under Anthropic’s own terms and privacy policy.

4. Retention and Control

Your credential remains until you disconnect. Cached usage remains until it is replaced, you disconnect, or App data is removed. Preferences remain until App data is removed. Diagnostics remain until you clear them or remove App data. Use Disconnect to remove the credential and cached usage, and Diagnostics → Clear to erase the local log. You can also revoke the Claude session through your Claude account. Uninstalling removes App data subject to standard macOS and Keychain behavior.

5. Security

ClaudGet uses the macOS App Sandbox, Keychain, and a least-privilege App Group shared only with its WidgetKit extension.

6. Third-Party Service

Your use of Claude remains subject to Anthropic’s Consumer Terms and Privacy Policy. ClaudGet is independent and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Anthropic. Claude and Anthropic are trademarks of Anthropic PBC.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated as the App changes. The effective date above identifies the latest version.
