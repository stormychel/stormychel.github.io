ClaudGet Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ClaudGet (“App”) handles information on macOS.

1. Information Processed

  • The App processes the Claude session you provide to request your usage information directly from Claude.
  • The session is stored in your macOS Keychain and is not shared with the widget.
  • Usage totals and reset times are cached locally in the App Group so WidgetKit can display them.
  • Diagnostic logs are stored locally and redact credentials.

2. Network Requests

The App sends your session only to Claude’s own web services to retrieve your account’s usage information. The developer does not operate an account service or analytics backend for ClaudGet.

3. Data Collection and Sharing

The developer does not collect, sell, rent, or share your personal data. ClaudGet contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

4. Retention and Control

Your credential, cached usage snapshot, preferences, and diagnostics remain on your Mac. Disconnecting removes the saved credential and cached usage. Uninstalling removes App data subject to standard macOS behavior.

5. Security

ClaudGet uses the macOS App Sandbox, Keychain, and a least-privilege App Group shared only with its WidgetKit extension.

6. Third-Party Service

Your use of Claude remains subject to Anthropic’s terms and privacy practices. ClaudGet is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated as the App changes. The effective date above identifies the latest version.
