Support

Need help with ClaudGet? Start with the checks below or contact the developer directly.

Sign-in trouble

  • Open ClaudGet Settings and reconnect your Claude session.
  • Confirm Claude works in your browser.
  • Open Diagnostics for a credential-redacted status report.

Widget not updating

  • Open ClaudGet and press refresh.
  • Keep the host app installed in Applications.
  • Remove and re-add the widget if macOS is showing an old extension snapshot.

Contact

Email stormychel@me.com. Include your macOS version and the Diagnostics output, but never send your Claude session or cookies.
