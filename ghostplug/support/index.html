Support

Need help with GhostPlug? Start with the checks below or contact the developer directly.

Licence keys

  • Your licence key is in the receipt email from your Gumroad purchase.
  • In GhostPlug's menu, choose Enter Licence… and paste the key in.
  • Lost the receipt? Email the address below with your Gumroad order number and we'll look it up.

The screen is unreadable

  • Wait: without a click on Keep the change is undone after the countdown, 10 seconds by default.
  • If you kept a resolution you now regret, choose Back to… in GhostPlug's menu.
  • If that resolution comes back at every launch and you can't use the screen, hold Option while GhostPlug starts: the restore is skipped.
  • Quitting GhostPlug always returns the Mac to its own display.

After a restart the resolution is not back

  • Turn on Launch at login in GhostPlug's Settings.
  • The Mac needs automatic login on and FileVault off; otherwise it waits at the login window and GhostPlug starts only once someone logs in.
