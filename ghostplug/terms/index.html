3. What GhostPlug does

GhostPlug creates a virtual display on your Mac at a resolution you choose and mirrors the Mac’s own displays onto it, so a Mac without a monitor, or one reached over Screen Sharing, can run at that resolution. Every change waits for you to click Keep and is undone if you do not.

It does this with a display interface that macOS provides but Apple does not document. A macOS update may change or remove it, and GhostPlug may stop working until it is updated, or for good.
