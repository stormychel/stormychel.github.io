ListSweep

Reset your checklists in one tap

ListSweep is a small utility that fills gaps Apple left in Reminders — starting with bulk-reset of completed items in a list. Everything stays on your device via EventKit.

  • Bulk-reset completed reminders
  • Swipe, multi-list, Siri & Shortcuts
  • Paid once on the App Store — no subscription
