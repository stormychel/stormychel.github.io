Android closed test

Try PawMode on Android before it launches

PawMode turns a spare phone into a pet monitor for your parked car. One phone stays in the car as the Monitor, the other stays with you and alerts you if the car gets too hot or the Monitor goes quiet. It is live on iPhone. On Android it is in closed testing, and Google needs twelve testers using it for fourteen days before it can go on Google Play for everyone. I build it on my own, so every tester counts.

  1. Join the tester group

    Sign in with the Google account you use on the Play Store on your phone, then join the group. That is what puts you on the test list. No form, nothing else to fill in.

    Join the group

  2. Opt in to the test

    Open the test page with the same Google account and tap Become a tester.

    The test opens in the next few days, once Google has finished reviewing it. Until then the test page says the app is not available. Join the group now, and I will email the group the moment the test is open.

    Become a tester

  3. Install it from Google Play

    PawMode now shows up in the Play Store like any other app, and updates arrive the same way. Set up one phone as the Monitor and one as your Personal phone, pair them with the QR code, and try it on a short stop.

    Open in Google Play

Please stay for fourteen days

Google counts testers who stay opted in for fourteen days in a row. Leaving early does not cause you any trouble, but it means one more tester has to be found before the launch. You can uninstall and leave the test at any time after that.

What is free

Everything in the Android test is unlocked while it runs, Premium features included.

What you need

Two phones: an Android phone with Google Play, and a second phone to pair it with. That one can be another Android phone or an iPhone running PawMode from the App Store. Either phone can be the Monitor.

An honest note

This is a test build. PawMode gives an indication of conditions in the car, not a guarantee, so please never rely on it alone to keep a pet safe, and certainly not while it is being tested.

Your address

Joining the group shares your Google account address with me, the group owner. It is used only to give you access to the test and to email you about it, such as when it opens. It is never shown to other members, never shared, and never used for anything else. Leave the group and it is gone. For anything else, see the privacy policy.

Found something?

Tell me at michelstorms@icloud.com. Which phones you paired and what the alert did, or didn't do, is the most useful thing a tester can send.
