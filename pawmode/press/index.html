The story

Why it's different

A handful of cars ship with a built-in pet mode — tied to that one car. PawMode brings the same reassurance to any vehicle, using a spare iPhone you already own.

  • No special car, no extra hardware. A spare iPhone becomes the in-car Monitor; the phone in your pocket is the remote dashboard.
  • Two phones, paired by QR, synced through your own Apple account. No sign-up, no third-party servers, no Bluetooth pairing dance — they check in every minute, even backgrounded.
  • Thermal state + repeating alerts. Color-coded warnings and push notifications the moment conditions cross a threshold, the battery runs low, or the car phone drops offline.
  • A reassurance message for passersby — with your contact and a scannable code — that auto-translates to the local language. Fewer broken windows, fewer worried calls.
  • Doubles as a deterrent. A visible surveillance overlay (and a stealth mode that blanks the screen while the camera keeps running) helps protect belongings, not just pets.
How it works

Two devices, one pairing

Monitor

The phone in your vehicle

Live front-camera feed, thermal-state display, a reassurance message for passersby, a contact QR code, video recording and timed auto-snapshots. Keeps the screen awake and auto-dims after inactivity; a passcode lock prevents tampering.

Personal

The phone you carry

A remote dashboard with thermal state, battery level, charging status and sensor readings. Request snapshots, edit the displayed message, and get push alerts the instant conditions change or the Monitor drops offline.
