PickDatePlace Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PickDatePlace ("App") handles information when you use the app on Apple platforms (iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch and iMessage) and the web voting page at pickdateplaceapp.com.

1. Data We Process

  • Poll content you enter: titles, descriptions, date/time options and place options.
  • Responses: the availability you select for each option and, if you enter one, a display name.
  • Identifiers: an anonymous iCloud user identifier for the account you vote or create polls with (when signed in to iCloud), and an app-generated device identifier (Apple's identifier for vendor on iPhone and iPad, a random identifier on Mac and in the browser). These are not your Apple ID, email address or phone number.
  • Purchase and entitlement information provided by Apple StoreKit.

2. How Data Is Used

We use data only to operate core app functionality: creating polls, recording responses, syncing polls between your devices, the iMessage extension, the widget, Apple Watch and the web voting page, recognising the organizer of a poll, and validating paid organizer access. The identifiers make sure a person's vote counts once, whether they vote from iPhone, iPad, Mac or the web, and let them change their vote later. They are not used for advertising or tracking.

3. Data Sharing and Visibility

Polls and responses are stored in Apple's CloudKit public database so that everyone invited can see them. Anyone who has a poll's link can read that poll, its responses and display names, and the identifiers stored with each response. Share poll links only with the people you are planning with.

We do not sell personal data and do not share it with third parties for advertising. CloudKit and payments are provided by Apple, and Apple processes related data under its own terms and privacy policies.

4. Data Retention

Poll and response data is stored on your devices and in CloudKit. Deleting a poll in the app, or uninstalling the app, removes the copy on that device; the shared copy in CloudKit stays available to people with the link. To have a poll or response removed from CloudKit, contact us.

5. Security

We use platform-provided protections (Apple frameworks and app sandboxing). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

PickDatePlace is rated 4+ on the App Store. We do not knowingly collect personal data online from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data in connection with the App, contact us and we will review and remove it where appropriate.

7. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top indicates the latest version.
