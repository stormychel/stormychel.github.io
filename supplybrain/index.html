iPhone & iPad

SupplyBrain

Keep stock of your fridge, freezer, pantry and any container you name. Snap a product and your device fills in the name, weight and best-before date.

Join the beta

Coming soon to the App Store. Requires iOS 26 or iPadOS 26.

Features

Know what you have, before you shop

Photos do the typing. Your data stays yours.

Snap to add, snap to remove

Point the camera at a product. Apple Vision and on-device Apple Intelligence fill in the name, amount, weight and best-before date. Took something out? Snap it and SupplyBrain matches the stock line for you.

Remembers your products

Snap the front for the name and the back for the weight and date. The next time you snap the same product, it fills in at once.

Share with a QR code

Share a container with a QR code or an invite link. It works across Apple IDs, is end-to-end encrypted over CloudKit, and needs no accounts.

Expiry reminders

Get a reminder before things expire, or see what is running out at a glance with the Expiring soon widget and the Lock Screen count.

Widgets for every container

Put a widget on your Home Screen for any container, with Snap to add and Snap to remove one tap away.

Private by design

No accounts, no servers, no tracking. Recognition runs on your device and nothing is collected. In English and Dutch.
