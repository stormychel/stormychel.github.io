SupplyBrain Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

SupplyBrain keeps stock of what is in your fridge, freezer, pantry and other containers. It is built so that your data stays yours.

What the app stores

Container names, item names, amounts, weights, expiry dates, notes and small product thumbnails you add. This data is stored on your device and, when you are signed in to iCloud, in your private iCloud database so it syncs between your devices.

Photos and on-device recognition

When you snap a product, the photo is analysed on your device using Apple's Vision framework and, where available, Apple Intelligence (Foundation Models). Photos are never sent to us or to any third party. Only a small thumbnail is kept with the item if you save it, and that thumbnail syncs with your stock in your own iCloud.

Remembered products

So the next photo of the same product fills in its details, SupplyBrain remembers products you add: the name, brand and amount you saved, the barcode or label text it read, and a small thumbnail. This is stored on your device and in your private iCloud database, never shared. Settings → Remembered products lists it, and you can forget one product or all of them.

Sharing

If you share a container, the app creates a QR code and invite link holding a random code and a secret key. The container and its items are then stored in SupplyBrain's public CloudKit database at Apple, encrypted on your device with that key, so Apple and we see only unreadable data. Anyone you give the QR code or link to can read and change that container until sharing is stopped. Stop sharing ends it for everyone; each person keeps the items they already have.

Notifications

Expiry reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No notification data leaves your device.

Purchases

SupplyBrain Plus is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details or Apple Account. The app only asks the App Store whether you have Plus. If you share a container, its encrypted data records until when your Plus covers it, so people you share with can use it without limits.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no email addresses.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
  • No servers of ours: the app talks only to Apple's iCloud and the App Store on your behalf.

Your control

Delete an item, a container or the app itself and the data is gone from your device. iCloud data can be managed in Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Storage.
