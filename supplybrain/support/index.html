SupplyBrain Support

Snap to add

Open a container, tap Snap to add, point the camera at the product label and tap the shutter. Check the recognised name, amount, weight and best-before date, then tap Add. Keep snapping to add more.

Snap to remove

Tap Snap to remove, snap what you took out, confirm the matching stock line and how much came out.

Recognition quality

Recognition runs on your device. With Apple Intelligence enabled (Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri) the app understands labels best; without it, label text and image recognition still work. Good light and a label filling the frame help a lot. Everything can be corrected before saving.

Sharing a container

Open the container and tap Share. A QR code appears: the other person scans it with the Camera app on their iPhone or iPad, or you tap Send invite link. On a Mac, open the link there, or paste it under Join with invite link (the link icon next to +). Everyone who joined can add, take out and edit; changes show up within seconds while the app is open. Both sides need SupplyBrain and to be signed in to iCloud. Stop sharing ends it for everyone.

SupplyBrain Plus

SupplyBrain is free for up to 30 items, 3 containers and 10 photo scans. SupplyBrain Plus removes these limits and lets you share containers. It comes as a monthly or yearly subscription (the yearly plan starts with a 7-day free trial) or a one-time lifetime purchase, all with Family Sharing. One person with Plus covers a shared container for everyone in it. Manage or cancel a subscription in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions; restore a purchase from SupplyBrain's Settings.

Expiry reminders

Settings (gear icon) lets you choose how many days ahead to warn and whether to receive reminders. Reminders arrive at 9:00 local time.
