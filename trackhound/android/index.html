Ride TrackHound on Android before it launches

TrackHound plans routes onto the small roads, farm tracks and gravel that other apps steer you away from, and it works offline. It is live on iPhone. On Android it is in closed testing, and Google needs twelve riders using it for fourteen days before it can go on Google Play for everyone. I build it on my own, so every tester counts.

  1. Join the tester group

    Sign in with the Google account you use on the Play Store on your phone, then join the group. That is what puts you on the test list. No form, nothing else to fill in.

    Join the group

  2. Opt in to the test

    Open the test page with the same Google account and tap Become a tester. If it says the app is not available yet, give the group a minute to catch up and reload.

    Become a tester

  3. Install it from Google Play

    TrackHound now shows up in the Play Store like any other app, and updates arrive the same way. Download a region, plan a ride, and take it out.

    Open in Google Play

Please stay for fourteen days

Google counts testers who stay opted in for fourteen days in a row. Leaving early does not cause you any trouble, but it means one more rider has to be found before the launch. You can uninstall and leave the test at any time after that.

What is free

Everything in the Android test is unlocked while it runs. You need an Android phone with Google Play, and a region pack takes some storage.

Your address

Joining the group shares your Google account address with me, the group owner. It is used only to give you access to the test. It is never shown to other members, never shared, and never used for anything else. Leave the group and it is gone. For anything else, see the privacy policy.

Found something?

Tell me at michelstorms@icloud.com. A short note on where you rode and what the route did is the most useful thing a tester can send.
