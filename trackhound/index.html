iOS Offline Navigation

TrackHound

One offline nav engine, tuned to how you actually drive or ride. Pick Fastest for the commute, Curviest for Sunday roads, or Unpavediest for dirt and gravel — then make it your own. Routing runs entirely on your iPhone, so it keeps working with no signal.

Now on the App Store for iPhone and CarPlay — route planning is free.

Features

Built to fit how you drive

On-device routing, offline maps, and four route styles you can tune to any ride — no account, no server.

On-Device Routing

A full Valhalla routing engine runs on your iPhone. No backend, no tracking — routes calculate locally, even in airplane mode.

Offline Maps

Download map regions from a worldwide catalogue — hundreds of them. Plan and navigate with zero signal once a region is on the phone.

Unpavediest Mode

One of four styles: it hunts for gravel, dirt, and farm tracks and biases hard toward narrow, traffic-free roads — for when you want off the main drag.

Four Route Styles

Switch between Shortest, Fastest, Curviest, and Unpavediest with a tap — same start and end, four very different rides.

Compare on the Map

Run all four presets at once and see them overlaid side by side, colour-coded, so you can pick the line you actually want before you leave.

Roadbook & Live Map

Two full-screen turn-by-turn modes: a rally-style roadbook with a heading compass, or a moving map that follows you with speed, ETA, and the speed limit on-screen.

Loop Generator

Set a target distance and TrackHound builds a round-trip loop that brings you back to where you started — ideal for a free afternoon.

GPX Import & Export

Bring in tracks from elsewhere or export your planned route as GPX to share it or load it onto a dedicated GPS.

CarPlay

TrackHound's moving map, maneuver card, and instrument tiles — speed with the limit sign, heading, attitude, and altitude — run on your car's screen, plus a live Dashboard card. Turn-by-turn on CarPlay needs Pro and switches on once Apple approves the navigation entitlement.
