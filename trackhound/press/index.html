The story

Why it's different

Most apps optimise for speed or distance. TrackHound optimises for the opposite — the small, narrow, traffic-free roads that make a ride worth taking.

  • Existing apps (OsmAnd, Komoot, Scenic, Google Maps, Gaia) optimise for speed or distance. TrackHound optimises for the opposite: small roads, farm tracks, dirt paths.
  • "Farm road" isn't an OSM tag. A Belgian farm road is often perfectly paved — just narrow and nearly traffic-free. "Prefer unpaved" filters miss these because they filter on surface, not road class or traffic. TrackHound's costing model targets the character of the road, not just its surface.
  • Compare All draws every routing preset on the map at once, so you pick the ride you want.
  • Truly offline & private: download a region once, route on-device in airplane mode; routes and trips stay on the phone. No tracking, no analytics, no ads.
  • Rally-style roadbook: glance-readable maneuver arrow, route-alignment compass (green on course, red when you drift), speed-limit-coloured speedo — portrait and landscape.

What's in it

Free to plan, Pro for the road

Free

Route planning is always free

  • Four presets — Shortest, Fastest, Curviest, Unpavediest
  • Compare All — every preset drawn on the map at once
  • Fine-grained costing controls per ride
  • Closed-loop route preview from a target distance + start point
  • Download your first region; calculate routes on-device, offline
  • Export any route as GPX
  • Worldwide address search, pick-from-Contacts, long-press to set start/destination

TrackHound Pro

Subscription or one-time · annual has a 14-day free trial

  • Roadbook turn-by-turn navigation (maneuver arrow + distance + instruction, alignment compass, speed-limit-coloured speedometer, portrait & landscape)
  • Navigate, save and export closed-loop rides
  • Import GPX from Files or Mail
  • Download additional regions beyond the first
  • Save custom route presets
  • CarPlay navigation (activates once Apple approves the navigation entitlement)
