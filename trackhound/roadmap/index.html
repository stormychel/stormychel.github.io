Roadmap

What's coming to TrackHound

Planned work, grouped by theme. These are planned, not yet shipped — priorities and timing can change.

The live backlog is the GitHub issue tracker.

Navigation & guidance

  • Colour the route line per leg, by each leg's routing type #675
  • Compare All — add your active custom preset as a fifth option #611
  • Smarter loop generation — no dead-end spurs or self-crossings #654 #652
  • Faster Curviest / Unpavediest calculation #451

Routing modes & vehicles

  • More modes: MTB #216, Hiking #213, Quad/ATV #217, Horse #214
  • Scooter — hybrid ride/walk with mount & dismount prompts #685
  • Vehicle info + range → fuel/charging stops along a route #38 #39

Trip planning

  • Ride breaks — split a route into km / mile / time sections for fuel & rest stops #678, incl. EV charger stops #680
  • Camping / overnight stops along a route #37
  • Group rides — ride together, everyone live on the map #574
  • Community POIs — rider-contributed viewpoints, camp spots, trailheads #288
  • Route padding: loops to a target duration & compare-all compositions #264
  • Next-hour weather nowcast #112

CarPlay, Watch & more devices

  • Apple Watch — full turn-by-turn #585 and standalone cellular recording #584
  • CarPlay: find fuel/food along the route #589, full roadbook mode #425
  • Home-screen & CarPlay Dashboard widgets #668 #341
  • iPad #343, Mac planning #90, Siri / App Intents #357

Safety & data

  • Speed-camera awareness — fixed + mobile/live community alerts #58 #413
  • Average-speed / section checks (trajectcontroles) #481
  • STOP / give-way / signal data for crossroads warnings #154

Platform & reach

  • Android — Valhalla NDK #321, Play + billing #323, Android Auto #325
  • Online routing when you have signal #238, plus a no-download Lite fallback #454
  • Richer offline search: full address/POI gazetteer #344, house-number precision #632; true offline basemap packs #366
  • Accessibility: VoiceOver #88 #499, Voice Control #500

This roadmap is a direction, not a promise — items ship, shift, or merge as the app evolves. For the always-current backlog, follow the issue tracker; to suggest or vote on something, open an issue.
